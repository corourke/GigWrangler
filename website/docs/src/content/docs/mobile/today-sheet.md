A gig's **day sheet** puts what you need on one screen: the run of day, where the venue is, who to call, and the gig's documents. It keeps working when the venue has no signal.

Staff, Managers and Admins have day sheets; Viewers don't. They're phone screens: on a computer, the address takes you to the dashboard.

## Opening a day sheet

- **For today's gig:** it's at the top of [Home](/mobile/home/), the first tab in the phone's bottom bar. Tap the gig, or **Open day sheet**.
- **For any gig:** on the gig's phone page, tap **Day sheet**. It works for any gig you can open, past or future.
- **For another gig:** on Home, tap **Choose another gig**:
  - Staff see the gigs they're staffing: upcoming ones and the last 30 days.
  - Admins and Managers see their organization's gigs, with a search box.
  - Gigs saved on the phone are marked.

  Tap a gig to open its day sheet.

The top of the sheet shows the gig's title, date and times, its status, and when the sheet was last saved on the phone, such as "Saved Nov 12, 3:00 PM". **Open gig** goes to the full gig page (online only). **Refresh** loads it again.

## What's on it

### Schedule

The gig's [schedule](/gigs/schedule/), in the gig's time zone: load-in, soundcheck, doors, sets and the rest. An item with an end time reads "to 6:00 PM". With nothing scheduled you see "No schedule yet."

### Venue

The venue's name, address and phone number, shown as text you can select and copy. **Directions** opens Apple Maps on an iPhone, iPad or Mac, and Google Maps elsewhere. **Call** rings the venue.

### Contacts and Crew

**Contacts** lists the contacts of the venue, act and other organizations on the gig. **Crew** lists the people staffing it, with their positions. Each person has **Call**, **Text** and **Email** buttons where they have a number or email, and the number is also shown as text.

There's no money on the sheet: no rates, fees or payments, and no gig notes.

### Documents

The gig's [attachments](/gigs/documents-and-notes/), such as a stage plot, rider or parking pass. Tap one to open it in a full-screen viewer: images and PDFs show in the app. Other files say "This file can't be shown here. Use Share to open it in another app." and offer **Share**. **Close** returns to the sheet.

A document saved on the phone says "Saved on this phone · 1.2 MB" underneath. Any other shows **Save for offline**: tap it to save that document.

## Saving a day sheet on the phone

To keep a gig's day sheet for when you're offline, open it and tap **Save on this phone**. The sheet is saved along with its PDFs and images, and the button turns into **Saved ✓** with **Remove** next to it. A saved sheet stays on the phone until you tap **Remove**, or until 30 days after the gig ends.

The sheet for today's gig, and any gig in the next 24 hours, is also saved automatically: each time you open it online, and in the background from any phone screen. A sheet saved automatically is removed a day after the gig ends, unless you saved it yourself.

PDFs and images up to 10 MB are saved along with the sheet. Larger files, and other kinds such as Word documents, are saved only when you tap **Save for offline**. A gig's saved documents can take up to 50 MB, and all gigs together up to 150 MB. When the phone has no more room, you see "Not enough room on this phone for this gig's documents. Remove a saved gig first." Other refusals say why: "This gig's saved documents would pass 50 MB", "Not enough room on this phone for it", or "Couldn't download it".

## Offline

Offline, the screen shows an **Offline** badge and the sheet as last saved, with its "Saved" time. Saved documents open as usual. A document that isn't saved reads "Not saved on this phone – connect to open". If the gig's sheet was never saved, you see "This gig's sheet isn't saved on this phone. Connect to load it."

Signing out removes every saved sheet and document.
