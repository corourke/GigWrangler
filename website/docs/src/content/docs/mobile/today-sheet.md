## Cover

- Opening **Today**: where it is, and which gig it picks ("the current gig"): rules for no gig
  today, several gigs, all-day gigs and gigs past midnight (dates in the gig's time zone).
- What it shows: the schedule (link [Schedule](/gigs/schedule/)), venue and directions, one-tap
  contacts (call, text, email), and the gig's documents.
- Who sees what (roles; staff vs Admins and Managers; any money hidden).
- Offline: what's saved for offline use and when; the Offline badge (link
  [Offline access](/mobile/offline-access/)).
