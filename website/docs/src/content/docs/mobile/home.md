The first tab in the phone's bottom bar is your home screen: the gig you're working today, anything that needs you, and the week ahead. Staff, Managers and Admins have it; Viewers start on **Gigs**.

For Staff the tab is called **Home**, and the bottom bar is **Home · Scanning · Settings**. Admins and Managers see it as **Today**. <!-- check: tab name for Admins and Managers -->

## Today's gig

At the top is a card for the gig you're working today, such as "Today · 7:00 PM", with the venue. With more than one gig today there's a card for each. With none today, the card shows the next one, such as "Next up: Sat 14 Nov". Tap a card, or **Open day sheet**, to open its [day sheet](/mobile/today-sheet/).

"Today" is the gig's date in its own time zone, so a gig at 11 PM shows on its own day, and an all-day gig counts for its whole date. Staff see the gigs they're staffing; Admins and Managers see their organization's gigs.

**Choose another gig** opens a list of gigs, so you can open any gig's day sheet. See [Opening a day sheet](/mobile/today-sheet/#opening-a-day-sheet).

## Needs you

**Needs you** counts the things waiting for you. A row appears only when its count is above zero; tap it to see the list.

| Row | What it counts | Who sees it | Tap opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Invites to reply to** | Gigs you've been asked to work and haven't answered yet, in any organization. | Staff, Managers, Admins | The gigs, with **Accept** and **Decline**. |
| **Open crew spots** | Places still to fill on your organization's gigs in the next 14 days. A place someone declined counts as open. | Managers, Admins | The gigs with open places; tap one to staff it. |
| **Access requests** | Requests waiting for a decision. Managers count requests to join; Admins also count requests for more access. | Managers, Admins | The requests on **Team**. |
| **Receipts to review** | Receipts and invoices in the scan queue not yet saved or discarded. | Managers, Admins | A list; you review them on a computer, as before. See [Capturing receipts on your phone](/mobile/receipt-capture/). |
| **Payments due or overdue** | Unpaid lines, money in or out, due today or earlier, shown as "2 due today · 1 overdue". | Admins | A list of the lines; tap one to open that gig's money. |
| **Equipment conflicts** | Gigs in the next 7 days with equipment booked in two places at once. | Managers, Admins | The gigs with conflicts. |

Staff see only **Invites to reply to**.

The gig card shows straight away; the counts load underneath it, the equipment one last. If a count can't load, it says "Couldn't load" and the others still show. Pull down, or tap ⟳, to load them again.

## This week

**This week** lists the gigs in the next 7 days, one line each, with the date and status. Staff see the gigs they're staffing. Tap one to open its gig page.

## Offline

Without a connection, Home still shows today's gig and **This week** from the copy saved on the phone. **Needs you** shows "Counts need a connection."
