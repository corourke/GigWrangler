Snap a receipt on your phone the moment you get it, and review it later on a computer. The phone only captures: the receipt goes into your organization's scan queue, it's scanned straight away, and you check it and save the purchase on the computer, as with any [scanned invoice](/financials/receipts-and-invoices/).

Admins and Managers can capture receipts. Staff and Viewers don't see the buttons.

## Capturing a receipt

There are two places to start:

- **For the organization:** tap the camera button in the header of the phone's **Gigs** list.
- **For a gig:** on the gig's phone page, tap **Capture receipt**. The receipt is linked to that gig.

Then, in the sheet that opens:

1. Tap **Take photo** to use the camera, or **Choose file** to pick photos or PDFs already on the phone. You can choose several at once.
2. Each receipt shows **Queued** and starts scanning straight away.

Photos are made smaller on the phone before they're sent, and HEIC photos from the photo library are converted, so a large photo is fine. A PDF can be up to 10 MB.

## Your recent receipts

Under the buttons, the sheet lists the receipts you've queued in the last 7 days, each with its status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Waiting** | Queued, not scanned yet. |
| **Scanning** | Being read. |
| **Ready to review on desktop** | Scanned. Review it on a computer. |
| **Failed** | The scan didn't work. Retry it on a computer. |

**Remove** deletes a receipt and its file. It's only on your own receipts that are still **Waiting**. Nothing else can be changed on the phone.

## Reviewing on a computer

On a computer, open **Financials → Purchases → Scan invoices**. Your receipts are in the **Scan queue** with the rest of the organization's, and the first one that's ready opens for review. Check it and select **Save Purchase**, as described in [Receipts & invoices](/financials/receipts-and-invoices/#1-financials--purchases--scan-invoices). A receipt captured from a gig opens already linked to that gig.

There's no review on the phone.

## Without a connection

If the phone is offline, receipts you capture are kept on the phone and upload when it's back online. Then they scan as usual.
