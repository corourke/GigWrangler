Snap a receipt on your phone the moment you get it, and review it later on a computer. The phone only captures: the receipt goes into your organization's scan queue, it's scanned straight away, and you check it and save the purchase on the computer, as with any [scanned invoice](/financials/receipts-and-invoices/).

Admins and Managers can capture receipts. Staff and Viewers don't see the camera button.

## Capturing a receipt

There are two places to start, both a camera button:

- **For the organization:** the camera button in the header of the phone's **Gigs** list.
- **For a gig:** the camera button on the gig's phone page. The receipt is queued as a cost of that gig.

The **Capture receipt** sheet opens. It says where the receipt goes: "Goes to the scan queue. Review it on desktop under Purchases.", or from a gig, "Goes to the scan queue as a cost of *gig*."

1. Tap **Take photo** to use the camera, or **Choose file** to pick photos or PDFs already on the phone. You can choose several at once.
2. While they upload you see "Sending 1 of 2…", then "Queued 2 receipts". Each one starts scanning straight away.

A receipt can be a photo (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF) or a PDF. Photos are made smaller on the phone and saved as JPEG before they're sent, and HEIC photos are converted, so a large photo is fine. A PDF can be up to 10 MB.

If a file can't be used, you see one of:

- "*file* is over 10 MB. Receipts can be up to 10 MB."
- "*file* isn't a photo or a PDF."
- "*file* can't be read here. Take the photo with the camera, or choose a JPEG, PNG or PDF."

## Your recent receipts

Under the buttons, **Queued in the last 7 days** lists the receipts you've captured, newest first, with how long ago, the gig if it has one, and its status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Waiting** | Queued, not scanned yet. |
| **Scanning…** | Being read. |
| **Ready to review on desktop** | Scanned. Review it on a computer. |
| **Failed** | The scan didn't work. Retry it on a computer. |

**Remove** (the bin) deletes a receipt that isn't being scanned. You're asked first: "Remove *file*? The file is deleted and no purchase is saved." It disappears from the scan queue on the computer too. Nothing else can be changed on the phone.

## Reviewing on a computer

On a computer, open **Financials → Purchases → Scan invoices**. Your receipts are in the **Scan queue** with the rest of the organization's: the first one that's ready opens for review, and **Review** on a row opens that one. Check it and select **Save Purchase**, as described in [Receipts & invoices](/financials/receipts-and-invoices/#1-financials--purchases--scan-invoices).

A receipt captured on a gig's page is listed as "*file* for *gig*", and opens with **Add to the costs of *gig*** ticked, so saving it also adds the gig expense. Untick it to save the purchase without the gig.

There's no review on the phone.

## Without a connection

When the phone is offline, the sheet says "You're offline. Receipts are kept on this phone and sent when you're back online." **Take photo** and **Choose file** still work: you see "Kept 2 receipts on this phone. They send when you're back online." The same happens if the connection drops partway through sending.

Kept receipts are listed under **On this phone** as **Waiting to send**, or **Couldn't send** if sending failed. They're named by the time you took them, such as receipt-20261109-192119-16ec75.jpg, and keep that name on the computer. **Remove** deletes one from the phone so it's never sent.

When the phone is back online, or you open the sheet while online, kept receipts are sent and move to **Queued in the last 7 days**. Then they scan as usual.

A phone can keep up to 20 receipts (50 MB) for you. Past that you see "This phone is holding 20 receipts to send. Connect to send them before adding more."

Signing out deletes any receipts still waiting on the phone. They aren't sent.
