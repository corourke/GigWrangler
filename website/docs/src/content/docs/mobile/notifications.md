GigWrangler can send an alert to your phone, or to a computer's browser, when something needs you: a request to work a gig, a reply to one you sent, a change to a gig you're working, a request to join your organization, or a payment that's due. Each alert is also a notification in the bell (top right), so nothing is lost if you don't turn alerts on.

## Turning notifications on

Alerts are turned on for each device separately, in **Settings → Notifications**:

- **On a computer:** the avatar menu → **Settings**, then the **Notifications** tab.
- **On a phone:** tap **Settings** in the bottom bar. **Notifications** is a card near the top.

Under **This device**, tap **Turn on notifications on this device**. Your browser or phone then asks whether to allow notifications: allow them. GigWrangler only asks after you tap the button. You see "Notifications are on for this device".

**On an iPhone or iPad**, alerts only work in the app opened from the Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or later), not in a Safari tab. In a Safari tab the card says: "To get notifications on an iPhone or iPad, open GigWrangler from your Home Screen: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen." Do that, open GigWrangler from the new Home Screen icon, and turn notifications on there.

**This device** shows where the device you're using stands:

| It says | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Get a notification on this device when something needs you. | Off. **Turn on notifications on this device** turns it on. |
| Notifications are on for this device. | On. **Turn off on this device** turns it off. |
| Notifications are blocked for GigWrangler in this browser's settings. Allow them there, then come back here. | You (or the browser) said no to notifications. Allow them for GigWrangler in the browser's or phone's settings. |
| This browser can't show notifications. | Use another browser, or on an iPhone the Home Screen app. |

## Your devices

**Devices with notifications on** lists every phone and browser where you've turned them on, such as "iPhone · Safari" or "Mac · Chrome", with when it was last notified. The one you're using is marked **This device**. **Remove** turns alerts off on another device, for example a phone you no longer have.

You can have up to 10 devices. Past that you see "Notifications are on for 10 devices already; remove one in Settings first".

Signing out turns alerts off on that device. If someone else signs in on the same phone, it gets their alerts, never yours.

## What you're notified about

Under **Notify me about** there's a switch for each kind of alert. You see the ones that apply to your roles in any of your organizations. They're all on to start with, and they apply to all your devices. Turning one off stops the alert, but the notification still appears in the bell.

| Switch | When | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| **Gig requests** | You're asked to work a gig. | The person asked. |
| **Gig changes** | A gig you're working changes date, time or venue, or is cancelled. | The gig's staff who have been asked or have confirmed, except whoever made the change. |
| **Staffing replies** | Someone accepts or declines a gig. | Admins and Managers of the organization that asked them. |
| **Access requests** | Someone asks to join your organization, or asks for more access. | Requests to join: Admins and Managers. Requests for Manager or Admin access: Admins. |
| **Payments** | Payments are due or overdue. | Admins. |

Staff and Viewers see **Gig requests** and **Gig changes**. Managers also see **Staffing replies** and **Access requests**. Admins see all five.

Some details:

- **Gig changes** are gathered up: several edits close together send one alert, about two minutes after the last edit (at most 15 minutes after the first). Other edits, such as the title or notes, don't send one. A gig that's already over doesn't.
- **Payments** come as one summary a day, in the morning in the US (15:00 UTC). It counts unpaid lines in both directions, money owed to you and bills you owe, that are due that day, plus any that became overdue in the past week. Each line is reminded once on its due date and once when it's overdue. The alert never shows amounts or names.
- You get at most 10 alerts an hour. Past that they wait in the bell.

## What the alerts say

| Alert | Title | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Gig request | New gig request | You've been asked to work Stage Hand · Sat, Oct 10 · Harvest Gala |
| Reply | Staffing reply | Sam accepted Stage Hand · Harvest Gala (or "declined") |
| Gig change | Gig changed | Harvest Gala: date and venue changed |
| Cancellation | Gig cancelled | Harvest Gala was cancelled |
| Access request | New access request | Sam asked to join Demo Sound & Lighting (or "asked for Manager access in …") |
| Payments | Payments | 2 payments are due today · 1 is overdue. Open GigWrangler for details. |

Alerts can show gig titles, roles and people's first names. They never show amounts, rates, email addresses or phone numbers.

## On the lock screen

To keep even titles and names off the lock screen, turn on **Hide details on the lock screen** under **Lock screen**. Every alert then reads only "GigWrangler: You have a new notification." It's off to start with, and it applies to all your devices.

## Tapping an alert

Tapping an alert opens GigWrangler on the screen it's about: the gig, the requests on **Team**, or **Financials** for payments. It switches to the right organization if needed, and marks the notification in the bell as read.
