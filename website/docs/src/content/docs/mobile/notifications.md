## Cover

- Turning notifications on: install the app to the home screen first (web push for the installed
  app; link [Mobile app & field operations](/mobile/overview/)), then allow notifications when asked.
  iPhone specifics (Home Screen app required).
- Where each alert can be switched on or off: **Settings** (which tab), per person.
- The alerts, and who gets each one:
  - Assignment invites: to the person invited.
  - Accept and decline replies: to the gig's Admins and Managers.
  - Gig changes to date, time, venue, or cancellation: to the gig's assigned staff.
  - Join and access requests: to Admins and Managers.
  - Payment due and overdue reminders: to Admins. The text carries no amounts or client names.
- What tapping an alert opens.
- Turning them off, and what happens on a new phone or after signing out.
- Relation to the in-app notification bell (see [The dashboard](/getting-started/the-dashboard/)).
